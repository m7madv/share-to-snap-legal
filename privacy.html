Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

Share to Snap: Gallery Tool helps users select photos or videos from their device gallery, preview them locally, and open Snapchat through supported sharing flows. This app is not affiliated with Snapchat.

Media Files

Selected photos and videos stay on the user's device. The app does not upload selected media to our servers. Media is handed to Snapchat only after the user taps the share/open action.

Information We Collect

The app may store local app preferences on the device, such as premium status or recent local selections. The app does not require account registration and does not collect names, email addresses, or Snapchat credentials.

Advertising

The app may show ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostics to provide and measure ads according to Google's policies.

Purchases

Premium purchases, if available, are processed by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store services. We do not receive or store full payment card details.

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with Snapchat, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Apple StoreKit. Use of those services is also governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Users should only share media they own or have permission to share.

Data Deletion

Because selected media remains on the user's device and no account is created with us, users can remove local app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support email listed on the app's store page.
