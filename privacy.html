Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Share to Snap: Gallery Tool is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Snapchat or Snap Inc.

What the app does

The app lets you choose a photo or video, preview it on your device, and then open Snapchat or the iOS share sheet. Sharing is always initiated and completed by you.

Photos and videos

The app uses the system media picker so you can select specific items. Selected media is processed locally and is not uploaded to a server operated by the developer. A Premium user may save references to up to five recent selections locally on the device. A selected item is provided to Snapchat or another destination only after the user chooses to share it.

Accounts and local data

The app has no developer-operated account system and does not ask for Snapchat credentials. Local preferences can include completed-share count, consent choices, recent-file references, and a short-lived cached Premium status. Removing the app removes its ordinary local data; secure operating-system storage may follow the device platform's own retention rules.

Advertising and consent

Free users may see banner and occasional interstitial ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads and its partners may collect or process data such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, advertising data, product interaction, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostics. Uses can include third-party advertising, ad delivery, fraud prevention, analytics, and measurement. Whether data is linked to a user or used for tracking depends on Google's configuration, the user's consent choices, region, and platform settings.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform where a consent or privacy-options form is required. The app does not itself request access to Apple's advertising identifier and does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt in the current version. Advertising is disabled while an active Premium entitlement is present.

Purchases and subscriptions

On iOS, the Annual Premium auto-renewable subscription and any introductory free trial are offered and processed by Apple through StoreKit. On Android, purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store full payment-card details. The app reads current store entitlements to grant or remove Premium access and keeps only a short offline cache on the device.

Analytics

No separate first-party analytics service is integrated. AdMob may perform advertising measurement and diagnostics as described above.

Third-party services

The app may interact with Snap, Google AdMob, Apple, and Google Play. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Users must follow the age and account requirements of the stores and services they use.

Your choices and support

You can review available ad privacy choices from Settings, manage an Apple subscription from Apple Account settings, remove recent-file references in the app, or uninstall the app. For questions, use the public support page.
