Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026. This policy applies to GalleryPath on Android, formerly named Share to Snap: Gallery Tool. GalleryPath is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Snapchat or Snap Inc.

Photos and videos

GalleryPath uses the system picker to let you choose specific photos or videos. Selected files are previewed and processed on your device, not uploaded to a developer-operated server. A file is provided to a receiving app only after you choose to share it. Premium can keep recent-file information locally for repeat use; you can clear recent items in the app.

Accounts and local storage

There is no developer-operated account system and we do not request Snapchat passwords. Local data can include preferences, completed-share counts, consent choices, recent-file information, and a short-lived cached Premium entitlement. Uninstalling removes ordinary local app data; secure storage follows Android's retention rules.

Ads, consent and diagnostics

Free users may see Google AdMob banner and occasional full-screen ads. Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, advertising data, product interaction, crash data, performance data and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and analytics. Data linking and personalized advertising depend on consent, region and platform settings. Google User Messaging Platform requests consent or offers privacy choices where required. Available privacy choices can be reviewed in app settings. Ads are disabled while Premium is active. No separate developer-operated analytics service is integrated.

Purchases

The optional monthly Premium subscription is processed by Google Play Billing and renews until cancelled. We read store entitlements to enable Premium and may cache status briefly on the device. We do not receive full payment-card details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

Third-party services

Sharing destinations and advertising or billing providers handle the data they receive under their own policies: Snap privacy and Google privacy. GalleryPath is not directed to children under 13. Follow the age requirements of the stores and services you use.

Your choices

You may clear recent items, review available ad privacy options, cancel a subscription through Google Play, or uninstall the app. For privacy questions use the support route below. Do not post private files or sensitive information in public support requests.

Support

Developer: SAMIR KHALED ALZOUBI. Open a support request with the GalleryPath version, Android version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. Requests on this service may be public. Do not attach personal photos, videos, passwords, receipts or payment details.

For purchases, use Restore Purchases while signed into the Google Play account used for purchase. Manage subscriptions through Google Play. For sharing, Snapchat must be installed when using the Snapchat destination; availability depends on device and service compatibility.

Terms of Use

You must have the right to share selected media and comply with the receiving service's rules. GalleryPath opens a supported sharing flow; it does not send files automatically. You review the destination and confirm sending in the receiving app. It cannot guarantee how another app displays media or whether a share counts toward a streak.

GalleryPath is independently developed and is not endorsed by Snap Inc. Snapchat and related names belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify the sharing destination. Premium billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled through Google Play. No paid subscription is required to use the ad-supported features.
